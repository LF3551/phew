# Commit with Phew

Click **✨** in the Source Control view to write a commit message from your changes.

In **Phew Settings → Commits**, you see the author and the signing that commits use, and you can set:

- Conventional Commits or plain messages, and the language.
- `Signed-off-by` (Developer Certificate of Origin).
- GPG or SSH signing: follow Git, always, or never.
- A different author for commits made with Phew.
- Extra trailers. Phew never adds co-authors by itself.
